Why we built this

Life is complex. Your tools shouldn't be.

Managing your money, health, taxes, travel and real estate shouldn't feel like a second job.

WizeLife was born from a simple, frustrating truth: the most important decisions in life — where to live, how to invest, when to see a doctor, whether that apartment is actually a good deal — require data that's scattered across dozens of apps, documents, and mental notes.

We kept asking the same question: why isn't there one intelligent place that pulls it all together?

So we built it. Not a feature, not a plugin — a full suite of AI-powered tools, each focused on a different domain of life, all connected under one roof. Every tool is designed to take raw, confusing data and turn it into a clear recommendation you can actually act on.

The goal has never changed: help people make better decisions, faster, with less stress — regardless of their financial background, language, or technical skill.

🛡️ Security

Security taken seriously, from day one.

This isn't a startup that ships first and hardens later. Strict security protocols are baked in from the beginning — not bolted on after a breach.

✓ HSTS preload ✓ App Check + reCAPTCHA ✓ Rate limiting ✓ Branch protection ✓ Daily automated audits ✓ No third-party data sharing

Your data is encrypted in transit, protected by Firestore rules that ensure only you can read your own documents, and never shared with third parties. We run daily automated security scans and treat every vulnerability report as a priority.
